I studied international relations in my first year, but my passion leads me to writing, photography, and filmmaking. My ambition is always to become a creative and film director, and one day I will have my films screened to audiences around the world.

I realized I need to learn more about the world's cultures and the constantly evolving industry of the arts, so I moved to Canada in pursuit of a bachelor's degree in Global Business and Digital Arts at the University of Waterloo.

Since then, I have been producing, writing, and directing my own short films, as well as working with clients in fashion at a low-budget level.
